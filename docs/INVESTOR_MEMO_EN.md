# SwimPay — Investor Memo (pre-seed)

> Every market figure below is sourced (2025), not estimated. House rule: no invented data.

## In one line
SwimPay is **the infrastructure that moves money fiat→fiat** across borders and through the corridors nobody serves. The customer pays and gets paid in **normal money**; we do the invisible plumbing in between.

## The problem (real, not theoretical)
Africa **already won local payments**: ~1.2 billion mobile-money accounts, **$1.43 trillion** moved in 2025 — two-thirds of the world's mobile-money flow. Nobody needs to "bank" Africa — the phone did it.

But the moment money has to **cross a border**, it falls back onto 1970s correspondent banking: **2–5 days**, **+2.5–3% FX**, remittances **over 8%** — on infrastructure that is **shrinking**: **127 African banks lost their international banking access in 2024–2025**. It's getting harder, not easier.

People have already voted: **"digital dollars" are 43% of crypto volume in Sub-Saharan Africa**, used precisely for cross-border transfers and to protect savings. **The demand is proven; compliant supply is missing.**

## What we do
An **API** that professionals — and tomorrow, machines — plug into to send and receive money, in volume, across the hard corridors. The buyer pays in normal money; the merchant receives normal money. In between, a **deterministic engine** routes value over the cheapest digital rail, **invisibly**, and handles **complex operations**: pay many recipients at once, split, route across opposed currencies.

## Why now
1. Banks are **abandoning** African corridors → the gap is widening, not closing.
2. **Digital dollars** have become Africa's fastest-growing money rail → our invisible engine already exists and is exploding.
3. The **machine economy** is being born: software agents are starting to pay for services (**$8B in 2026 → $1.5T in 2030**).

## Our moat (not speed — that's table stakes)
- **The corridors nobody serves** — e.g. **Russia↔Africa**: families and businesses cut off from normal money channels. Real demand, near-zero supply.
- **The mobile-money last mile** — reaching the *wallet*, not just a bank account; what neither banks nor crypto ramps cleanly own.
- **The future edge (the most defensible):** an AI pays for what it **cannot do itself** — a **human action** (verify on the ground, deliver, attest). Paying that human **in their local currency** is exactly what no crypto-native player can do. **Machine → human action → local fiat**: that's where our rail becomes a moat, not a commodity.

## Business model
- **~1%** per operation (below PSPs' ~3%, below remittances' 8%).
- **Segmented custody**: non-custodial segments **first** (light, fast), custodial segments **later** (capitalized, ring-fenced to prevent contamination).
- **Autonomous infrastructure**: software + automation → low opex, high margin. A company built to **generate more than it consumes**.

## What's already built (technical risk is behind us)
The **settlement core is built and tested**: double-entry ledger, operation state machine, rules engine (caps, KYC, screening, fraud), on-chain reconciliation **verified live on testnet**, multi-corridor cost quoting. Engineering risk is largely retired — **what remains is market access and the real rails.** *(Current stage: pre-revenue, pre-license.)*

## The ask & use of funds
The founder bootstraps the technical foundation and the company. A **pre-seed (~$100–300k, adjustable to scope)** takes us from "software ready" to **first revenue**:
- legal framework + compliance (AML program, legal opinion);
- licensed ramp partnership(s) (fiat in/out) + starting liquidity;
- first corridor live + first design-partner customers;
- 12–18 months of runway, small team.

## The risks (owned, with mitigation)
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Dependence on ramp partners | Start **non-custodial** (the customer brings the rail); ring-fence the custodial side |
| Liquidity for custodial corridors (e.g. Russia) | A **later**, capitalized segment, after validating an off-ramp that accepts the flow |
| Regulation | Segmented custody + compliance **as a technical control**, sequenced |
| Distribution | One corridor + design-partner customers **before** scaling |

## The prize
African cross-border **$329B (2025) → $1T (2035)** · mobile money **$1.43T/yr** · AI payments **$1.5T (2030)**. We don't need all of it: **a slice of one corridor is a real company; the AI↔local-fiat bridge is a generational one.**
